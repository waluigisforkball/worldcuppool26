⚽ The World Cup 2026 Pool
All 48 teams get drafted out among the players. Your teams earn you points as they win games and advance through the tournament. Most points when the Final ends wins the pool. Check back here any time to see where the standings sit.
The Draft
  • Every one of the 48 teams is assigned to exactly one player — no shared teams, no leftovers.
  • Teams are handed out by a randomized snake draft that spreads each person's teams across the 12 groups. Nobody gets a cluster from one group, so everyone ends up with a mix of contenders and long shots.
  • Each player gets the same number of teams. If 48 doesn't divide evenly by the player count, the leftover team(s) stay unassigned and belong to nobody — keeping every roster equal.
  • The draft runs once and locks before kickoff. No trading, no re-drafts.
Scoring
Points come from group-stage results and how far a team advances. Points stack — a team that goes all the way earns everything along the path.
Owning the champion is the single best prize — but on its own it's not enough to guarantee a win. If the rest of your teams flop, someone with steady results across their whole roster can still catch you. Depth matters as much as a superstar team.
